# CellSense iOS App Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 4, 2026

CellSense ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in the CellSense mobile application for iOS.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Use

### Local On-Device Processing
* **Rooftop Solar & Utility Details**: All inputs entered into the calculator (monthly utility bill, utility company, rate plan, solar status, backup preferences) are stored locally on your device and used solely to calculate your estimated battery payback period and ROI.
* **Document Scanner, Photos, and Files**: When you use the Camera Scan, Photos Library, or Files App to upload an electricity bill, the application extracts text locally on your device using Apple's built-in **Vision** and **PDFKit** frameworks. 
* **Zero Data Transmission**: **No document contents, images, bill amounts, or utility data are ever transmitted to or stored on external servers.** All processing is performed entirely locally on your device's CPU/GPU.

## 2. Information Sharing
Because we do not collect, store, or transmit any data, we do not share any personal or usage information with third parties.

## 3. Security
All data used in calculations remains isolated within the iOS app's local sandbox, inheriting the security protections of the iOS operating system.
