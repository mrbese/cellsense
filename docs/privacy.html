Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 4, 2026

CellSense ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we manage and secure information within the CellSense mobile application for iOS.

🔒 100% On-Device Processing

CellSense operates entirely locally on your device. All calculations, optical character recognition (OCR), and document parsing are performed directly on your iPhone. We do not run any backend servers, and we never collect, store, or transmit your utility statements, images, or calculation details.

1. Information Collection & Use

  • Calculation Inputs: Any inputs you provide (e.g. monthly bill charges, current electric utility company, specific rate plan, backup priorities) are stored locally in the application sandbox and used solely to run the ROI calculation.
  • Bill Scanning & Uploads: When you use the device camera, import a PDF file, or upload a photo of your electricity bill, the application extracts text locally using Apple's built-in **Vision** and **PDFKit** frameworks. The image data is processed in memory and deleted immediately after text extraction completes.

2. Information Sharing

Because CellSense does not collect or transmit any data, we do not share, sell, or disclose your information to any third parties or analytics providers.

3. Data Security

Your data is protected by the built-in sandbox security model of iOS, which prevents other applications from accessing CellSense's local files and calculations.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any personal information and is safe for use by individuals of all ages.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at your support email address.
